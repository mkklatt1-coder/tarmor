MOCs - If any of these answers are YES then an MOC is required!


Equipment & Technology

Does the change involve new, modified, or relocated equipment?

Are we changing the process conditions (e.g., pressure, temperature, flow rates) outside of the original design parameters?

Are we introducing new chemical substances or changing the supplier of existing chemicals?

Are we changing control systems, PLC software, or alarm setpoints?

Does the change involve replacing equipment with a different type, model, or material?


Procedures & Safety

Does the change impact existing Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), maintenance procedures, or safe work practices?

Will the change modify any safety-critical elements, such as interlocks, emergency shutdowns, or relief systems?

Does the change affect emergency response plans or evacuation routes?

Does the change affect any ISO standards?


Personnel & Organization

Are we changing the organizational structure, such as merging teams or restructuring?

Are we reducing staff, changing operator shift schedules, or utilizing contractors in new ways?

Does the change require new training or a different level of expertise for employees?


General Risk Factors

Is this a permanent change rather than a temporary one (e.g., bypass piping, temporary software patch)?

Could the change introduce new hazards that were not covered in the original Process Hazard Analysis (PHA)?

Will the change result in new or increased emissions to the environment?
